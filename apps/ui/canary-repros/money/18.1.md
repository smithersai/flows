# 18.1 — `/keys.list` renders nothing; the BYOK route does not exist

Origin: <https://canary.smithers.sh> (bundle `assets/index-BHHXuMoZ.js`)
Account: `codeplanesmithers`
Tested: 2026-08-19

## Checklist row

> **18.1** `/keys.list` shows keys masked. No full key is ever rendered,
> logged, or copied to the clipboard.

## Steps

1. Sign in on <https://canary.smithers.sh>.
2. `fetch("/api/user/byok-keys")` from the page.
3. Send `/keys.list` in the composer and wait.

## Expected

A `keys` card listing each configured provider with a masked preview, or an
empty keys card when there are none.

## Actual

`GET /api/user/byok-keys` answers **404 `404 page not found`**. The product
Worker claims the path and forwards it to `SMITHERS_CLOUD_API_BASE_URL`
(`https://api.jjhub.tech`), and the jjhub Go backend has no BYOK route at all —
`grep -rni byok --include=*.go` over `~/plue` returns nothing.

The UI shows **nothing**: no `[data-kind="keys"]` card, no transcript line, no
toast. The body text is byte-identical before and after the flow.

The masking bar in the row cannot be graded because no key material of any kind
is rendered. Nothing leaks, but nothing works either.

## Selectors and routes

- Route: `GET /api/user/byok-keys` (product Worker → `api.jjhub.tech`).
- Card: `[data-kind="keys"]` (never present).
- Source: `apps/ui/src/mainview/state/seams/KeysSeam.ts`, `apps/server/src/index.ts` platform-proxy allowlist.

## Root cause

The jjhub platform never shipped the BYOK key store the UI seam was written
against (`KeysSeam.ts` cites `multi src/smithersCloud/byokKeys.ts` as its
reference). The silence on top of it is the composer-path bug in 19.3.
