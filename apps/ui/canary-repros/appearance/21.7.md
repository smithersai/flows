# §21.7 — at 200% zoom the corner chrome covers transcript text

**Row:** `21.7` — "Zoom to 200% and confirm nothing is clipped or unreachable."

**Verdict:** FAIL.

**Target:** https://canary.smithers.sh
**Route:** `/` (chat surface)
**Selector:** `.corner-chrome` (the balance chip, the admin reset button, the
dark-mode toggle) over `.smithers-transcript` content
**Repro:** `bun canary-repros/appearance/21.7.ts` (exits 1 while the bug is present)

## Steps

1. Open https://canary.smithers.sh signed in, at a 1280x900 window.
2. Zoom the browser to 200% — equivalently, drive it at a 640x450 CSS viewport,
   which is what a 1280x900 window reports at 200%.
3. Look at the top-right of the chat column.

## Expected

Nothing clipped, nothing covered, every control reachable.

## Actual

The layout half of the row passes: no horizontal scrollbar, zero off-screen
controls, zero overflowing `overflow: hidden` boxes, and all 17 visible controls
are still tab-reachable and in view (measured; tab walk enumerated at 640x450).

What fails is occlusion. `.corner-chrome` is `position: absolute; z-index: 50`
pinned to the top-right of the **chat column**. At 1280px the column is narrow
and centred, so the chrome sits over empty space (`text under it: []`). At 640px
the column is the full width, so transcript text runs underneath it:

```
.corner-chrome:  [504, 12, 122, 30]
text under it:   [{"tag":"DT","text":"What happens","rect":[27,7,586,19]},
                  {"tag":"DD","text":"Smithers lists the untriaged issues one at a time with","rect":[67,26,546,38]}]
```

The screenshot shows the words "suggested lab**el and assign**ee" covered by the
balance chip and the two icon buttons.

Because the transcript scrolls under a pinned overlay, whatever text happens to
sit at y≈12–42 is unreadable — including the last line of a short transcript,
which does not scroll away.

## Note for the fix stage

`apps/ui/src/mainview/styles/chat.css` already has a `@media (max-width: 640px)`
block that re-pads the transcript. Reserving a top gutter for `.corner-chrome`
below ~900px — or letting the chrome participate in flow rather than overlaying —
closes this. The comment on `.corner-chrome` in `App.tsx` says it was moved off
the viewport onto the chat column precisely to stop it covering an open pane's
header; the same reasoning applies to the transcript at narrow widths.
