## What the code says about intent

`apps/ui/src/mainview/styles/chat.css` carries deliberate narrow-width rules:

- `@media (max-width: 900px)` (line 39)
- `@media (max-width: 640px)` (line 719) — re-pads the transcript and composer,
  narrows message bubbles to 86%, collapses `.connector-options` and
  `.repository-facts` to one column, reflows `.world-workspace` to a 148px rail
- `@media (min-width: 641px) and (max-width: 900px)` (line 756)

`index.html` ships `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width,
initial-scale=1.0">`.

So narrow layout was designed for down to about 640px. 390px was not.

## Recommendation (not a decision)

The evidence points at "desktop-only for the alpha": the CSS stops at 640px, the
touch targets are desktop-sized, and one affordance overflows the viewport. If
the owner rules mobile IN scope, the two concrete defects above are the first
two tickets.
