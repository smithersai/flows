# 5.15 — dismissing the surfaces dropdown with a pointer press moves focus

- **Origin:** https://canary.smithers.sh
- **Tested:** 2026-08-18, signed in as `codeplanesmithers`
- **Selectors:** `[data-flow="surfaces"]` (trigger), `.composer-menu-list` (`role=menu`), `.composer-menu-item` (`role=menuitem`)
- **Screenshot:** `/tmp/canary-chat-5.15.png`

## Steps

1. Sign in at https://canary.smithers.sh.
2. Focus the surfaces button and press ArrowDown to open the menu.
3. Click anywhere outside the menu.
4. Read `document.activeElement`.

## Expected

Row 5.15: "The composer menu (surfaces dropdown): ArrowDown opens, arrows move,
Enter invokes, Escape closes, **a pointer press outside dismisses it without
moving focus**."

## Actual

Four of the five clauses pass:

- ArrowDown opens the menu and moves focus to the first item (`Connect`).
- ArrowDown / ArrowUp move and wrap (`Connect` → `World` → `Connect`).
- Enter invokes the focused item (picking `World` switched to the World surface).
- **Escape closes the menu and restores focus to the trigger**
  (`aria-label="Surfaces"`) — the correct behaviour, and the control for this
  finding.

The pointer clause does not. In all three tries the menu dismisses but focus
jumps off the trigger onto whatever the click landed near:

```
click (60,120)   -> dismissed true; focus BUTTON ".composer-menu-item" -> DIV ".sui-msg-scroller-viewport"
click (1380,600) -> dismissed true; focus BUTTON ".composer-menu-item" -> DIV ".sui-msg-scroller-viewport"
click (700,60)   -> dismissed true; focus BUTTON ".composer-menu-item" -> DIV ".sui-msg-scroller-viewport"
```

Focus should land back on the surfaces trigger, as it does on Escape. As shipped,
dismissing by pointer restarts the Tab order from the transcript viewport.
