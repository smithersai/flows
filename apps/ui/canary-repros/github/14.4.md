# 14.4 — `/prs.review … approve` is refused by the platform and the app says nothing

Origin: https://canary.smithers.sh · tested 2026-08-19 · account `codeplanesmithers`
Repro: `bun apps/ui/canary-repros/github/14.4.ts` · screenshot `/tmp/canary-github-14.4.png`

## Steps

1. Sign in on https://canary.smithers.sh.
2. Open a landing in `codeplanesmithers/canary-sandbox` (the repro picks the
   first open one).
3. `/prs.review <n> comment …`      → works, the review appears on the card.
4. `/prs.review <n> request-changes …` → works, the review appears on the card.
5. `/prs.review <n> approve …`      → nothing happens.

## Expected

All three verbs either land or state why they did not.

## Actual

`comment` and `request-changes` both land and re-render the card. `approve`
produces no card change, no transcript message and no toast. The platform's own
answer is a clear, correct refusal:

```
POST /api/repos/codeplanesmithers/canary-sandbox/landings/3/reviews
-> 422 {"message":"author cannot approve their own landing request"}
```

That sentence is exactly what the user needs and never sees.

## Route and selector

- Flow: `prs.review`; card `[data-kind="pr"]`, Reviews section; the card's own
  buttons are `data-flow="prs.review"`.
- Route: `POST /api/repos/{o}/{r}/landings/{n}/reviews` with
  `{"type":"approve", …}`.

Shared root cause with 13.4 / 14.3 / 14.6 — see 13.4.md.

Secondary note (not the row's failure): reviews render their author as
`someone`, because plue's review rows carry `reviewer_id` and no login, and the
card does not resolve it.
